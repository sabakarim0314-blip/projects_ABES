TASK 15: Operational recommendations
======================================================================
1. Send SMS reminders for every appointment. No-show rate is 25.35% without a reminder versus 14.07% with one, a gap of 11.28 percentage points. Reminders cost little and protect doctor time.
2. Target Gastroenterology (highest no-show rate, 22.66%) with a second reminder 24 hours before the visit and a simple confirm/cancel reply, so freed slots can be offered to waiting patients.
3. Reduce waiting at Neurology (average 56.75 minutes). Review how slots are spaced and stagger arrival times; consider extra front-desk or triage support during its peak hours.
4. Add capacity on Tuesday (average 45.0 appointments per day, the highest of the week) and rebalance load for General Medicine, which has 118.8 appointments per doctor. Move some bookings to quieter days.
5. Give new-patient appointments longer slots, since new patients wait about 10.7 minutes more than follow-ups. Also review the 83 unusually long waits (above 81.0 minutes) to find the doctors and days where delays pile up. Together, 31.0% of attended visits wait over 45 minutes.

Note: This is an operational analysis only. No clinical or medical conclusions are made. Results are based on a synthetic dataset.
